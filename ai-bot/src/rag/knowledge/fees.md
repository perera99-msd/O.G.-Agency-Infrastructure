# Fees, Cost Transparency & Refund Policy

Q: What is O.G. Agency's policy regarding recruitment fees and payments?
A: O.G. Agency (SLBFE License No. 2751) operates under strict ethical recruitment standards and government regulations:
1. Zero Exploitative Charges: We do not charge unauthorized placement fees. For many corporate partner roles, employers provide free visa processing and airfare sponsorship.
2. Transparent Invoicing: All official administrative costs, credential verification fees, and immigration processing charges are clearly detailed before work begins.
3. Official Payment Channels Only: Payments must be made exclusively via bank transfer to O.G. Agency's authorized company bank account or paid directly at our official cashier counter (No. 586/3, Walgama, Nagahawatta Malwana) with an official stamped receipt. We never accept informal cash payments or third-party agent money transfers.

Q: What is the refund policy if a visa or service is delayed or rejected?
A: Under O.G. Agency's Terms of Service:
- Consulting, skills assessment, and credential verification fees are non-refundable once document processing has commenced.
- In the rare event that a candidate's visa application is rejected solely due to a document filing error on O.G. Agency's part, a full review will be conducted, and a partial refund or complimentary re-filing policy will apply.
- Third-party fees paid directly to external entities (such as medical clinics for GAMCA tests, embassy visa fee receipts, and police clearance departments) are subject to those external entities' respective non-refundable rules.

Q: How can I verify official fees or report an unauthorized payment request?
A: If any individual claims to represent O.G. Agency and requests informal money transfers, please report it immediately to our management at info@ogagency.lk or call +94 112 476 348 (WhatsApp: +94 776 636 64).
