# Recruitment Process & Workflow Timeline

Q: How does the international recruitment process work at O.G. Agency?
A: O.G. Agency follows a proven, transparent 5-step hiring journey:
- Step 01 (Tell Us What You Need): Candidates register online or in-office with their CV, skills, and aspirations. Corporate employers submit job roles, required skills, and timelines.
- Step 02 (Smart Candidate Matching): Our team searches our global talent database and shortlists candidates with the right skills, experience, and language proficiency.
- Step 03 (Credential Verification & Trade Testing): We verify educational diplomas, certificates, and work references with issuing institutions and previous employers. Candidates undergo practical trade tests where required.
- Step 04 (Paperwork, Medicals & Visa Processing): We manage full visa applications, embassy appointments, GAMCA/Wafid medical fitness checks, and legal compliance.
- Step 05 (Relocation, Arrival & Onboarding): We coordinate flight bookings, travel logistics, cultural orientation training, and airport reception so new hires settle in smoothly.

Q: How long does the recruitment and deployment process take?
A: The typical timeline from candidate shortlisting to foreign deployment ranges between 35 and 75 days, depending on the role, destination country immigration rules, and embassy appointment availability. Using our expedited visa and pre-verified credential pipeline can shorten this timeline by up to 25 days. Processing starts within 24 hours of onboarding.

Q: Can candidates and employers track application and visa status online?
A: Yes! O.G. Agency provides a secure online tracking dashboard and mobile application where candidates and employer clients can view real-time milestone updates on candidate selection, interview outcomes, document attestations, visa approvals, and flight schedules.
