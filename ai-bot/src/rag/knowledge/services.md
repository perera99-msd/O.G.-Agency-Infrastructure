# Services, Industry Sectors & Global Destinations

Q: What core services does O.G. Agency provide for job seekers and employers?
A: O.G. Agency provides an end-to-end recruitment and international talent placement ecosystem built around 4 core service pillars:
1. Smart Candidate Matching: Sourcing candidates from our global database, matching skills, experience, and language proficiencies, and conducting pre-screen interviews.
2. Visa & Work Permit Support: Complete immigration paperwork preparation, embassy appointment scheduling, labor law compliance, and biometric liaisons.
3. Credential Verification: Multi-tier authenticity verification of educational degrees, vocational certificates, and employment history with issuing institutions to protect against fraud.
4. Relocation & Onboarding: Travel booking, flight tracking, local orientation, cultural training, and 24/7 support for new hires.

Q: In which industries and job categories does O.G. Agency recruit?
A: We source, train, and place skilled and professional talent across major industry sectors including:
- Garment Manufacturing & Apparel: Tailors, pattern makers, production supervisors, machine operators.
- Construction & Engineering: Civil engineers, MEP supervisors, heavy equipment operators, steel fixers, electricians, welders, plumbers.
- Agriculture & Farming: Greenhouse technicians, harvest specialists, agricultural equipment handlers.
- Retail, Logistics & Transportation: Trailer drivers, delivery drivers, warehouse supervisors, supply chain assistants.
- Hospitality & Tourism: Executive chefs, line cooks, waiters, front desk executives, housekeeping staff, kitchen stewards.
- Healthcare & Caregiving: Registered nurses, allied health workers, elderly caregivers.
