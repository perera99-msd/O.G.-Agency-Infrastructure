# Visa Requirements, Medical Fitness & Document Verification

Q: What mandatory documents are required to apply for a visa and job placement through O.G. Agency?
A: Candidates must provide the following 8 mandatory documents for overseas job applications:
1. Valid International Passport: Minimum 1-2 years remaining validity with at least 4 blank visa pages.
2. Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV / Resume): Detailing education, technical qualifications, and comprehensive work experience.
3. Educational & Vocational Certificates: Original academic degrees, diplomas, and technical trade certification records.
4. Experience & Reference Letters: Service letters from previous employers verifying dates and job responsibilities.
5. Passport-Size Photographs: Clear photographs with white background matching destination country embassy specifications.
6. Police Clearance Certificate (PCC): Official certificate issued by the relevant government police department confirming no criminal record.
7. Medical Examination Fitness Certificate: GAMCA / Wafid medical fitness test approval for Gulf countries, or screening from accredited medical centers for Europe.
8. National Identity Document: Copy of National Identity Card (NIC / Aadhaar / National ID).

Q: What is the medical examination procedure for Gulf (GCC) and European work visas?
A: Medical clearance is mandatory before visa issuance:
- GCC Countries (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain): Candidates must undergo medical tests through GAMCA (Gulf Approved Medical Centers Association) / Wafid authorized clinics.
- European & Other Destinations: Medical examinations are conducted at designated embassy-accredited hospitals.
- Standard medical tests include chest X-ray, blood screenings, vision check, and general physical fitness evaluation.

Q: How are document attestation, Apostille, and credential verification handled?
A: O.G. Agency coordinates complete document legalization and verification:
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation for educational and civil certificates.
- Apostille and Embassy document attestation for European and overseas jurisdictions.
- Direct verification with issuing universities and past employers to guarantee 100% genuine credentials and protect candidate eligibility.
